## Your route through this module

**Before class:** M01–M17: locate your own project evidence and distinguish completed tests from gaps. If you need a refresher, tell the instructor before the practical.

Use the instructor-completed [lab sheet](../H-SETS-Class-Lab-Sheet.md) to identify your machines, accounts, versions, inputs and recovery route. Bring a folder for your own evidence; do not copy demonstration results as your work.

| Stage | Open / do | Check before moving on |
|---|---|---|
| First lesson: L35 | [Read the notes](01-Student-Notes.md), discuss the worked example, then use the matching [lab section](02-Guided-Lab.md) | Trace one business requirement through control, actual test and evidence; revise hypotheses when new evidence appears. |
| First assignment | Complete only L35 in the [workbook](03-Student-Workbook.md) | Include the named evidence and explain one result |
| Second lesson: L36 | Continue the notes and matching lab after the first checkpoint | Explain your own contribution and hand over project gaps with owners and next actions. |
| Second assignment | Complete L36 in the workbook | Correct feedback and record an independent variation |
| Portfolio milestone | P08/G4: capstone readiness, defence and professional handover | Link existing evidence; do not duplicate the same activity as extra hours |

**Completion checklist:** explain both checkpoints, submit both lesson assignments, preserve required allowed/denied or comparative tests, and record recovery plus unresolved issues. Completing this module does not automatically pass its project or gate.

Plan six class hours and six independent hours in the default teaching week. The independent hours include assignment and project work. Use the [weekly planner](../H-SETS-Weekly-Study-Plan.md) for deadlines and the [assessment guide](../H-SETS-Student-Assessment-Guide.md) for marks.

**H-SETS · L35/L36**

Prerequisites: M01–M17; G1–G3 passed or specific remediation scheduled. Six guided and six independent hours include assignments, portfolio review and P08 planning. Do not add a ninth project.

Read [notes](01-Student-Notes.md), complete [lab](02-Guided-Lab.md) and [workbook](03-Student-Workbook.md). Solutions and G4 instructions are separate.

M18 is an integrated checkpoint on the proposed 24-week route. P01–P07 corrections continue in the studio; fresh P08 investigation and final defence complete by week 24. G4 remains pending until its individual assessment and required recovery evidence pass. On the intensive 18-week route, explicitly schedule the same remaining workload and assessment inside week 18. Attendance at M18 is not graduation.
