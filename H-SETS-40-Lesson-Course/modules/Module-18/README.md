## Module overview

Connect the course topics into an architecture review and a defensible investigation of a supplied case.

## What you should be able to do

- Trace a business service and identify tested and untested dependencies.
- Separate confirmed events, hypotheses and unsupported conclusions.
- Present findings, response decisions and remaining work clearly.

## Preparation

The 18-week teaching route ends here. The original multi-week projects may continue after Week 18; they are not automatically completed by finishing the reading.

## Guided practice and independent work

Complete the Lesson 39 integration review and Lesson 40 supplied-case investigation. Review the briefs for the remaining integration projects and begin Project 10 when all its prerequisites are met. Keep the lesson case distinct from the longer Northstar Manufacturing project.

Use the [tool-lab index](../../practicals/labs/README.md) and [project index](../../practicals/projects/README.md) to open the relevant brief. A stage started this week is not the same as a completed lab or project. Record completed, deferred and not-run tasks accurately.

## Weekly checkpoint

Prepare: an integration review, the Lesson 40 investigation report and an evidence-based project progress record.

Explain the purpose of your work, the evidence supporting the result and one limitation or unresolved question. Where you tested a control, distinguish the expected result from the observed result. Use fictional or approved training data and keep credentials out of submitted evidence.
