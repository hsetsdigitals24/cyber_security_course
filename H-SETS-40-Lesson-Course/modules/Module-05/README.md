## Module overview

Control access to Linux files and use service and log records to explain system behaviour.

## What you should be able to do

- Evaluate user, group, mode and ACL permissions.
- Distinguish configured rights from effective access.
- Inspect a service and interpret relevant journal or authentication records.

## Preparation

Use approved fictional accounts and preserve the instructor management route. Tool Lab 02 hardening stages follow Lesson 14.

## Guided practice and independent work

Use the isolated Linux VM to practise the lesson user/group and access tasks. Compare an allowed operation with a refused operation. Inspect the assigned service and relevant logs. Preserve the original log records alongside your interpretation.

Use the [tool-lab index](../../practicals/labs/README.md) and [project index](../../practicals/projects/README.md) to open the relevant brief. A stage started this week is not the same as a completed lab or project. Record completed, deferred and not-run tasks accurately.

## Weekly checkpoint

Prepare: a permissions test table, service observations and a short log interpretation.

Explain the purpose of your work, the evidence supporting the result and one limitation or unresolved question. Where you tested a control, distinguish the expected result from the observed result. Use fictional or approved training data and keep credentials out of submitted evidence.
