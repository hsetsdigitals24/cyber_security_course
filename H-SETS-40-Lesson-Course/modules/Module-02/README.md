## Module overview

Connect information protection with identity checks and the permissions required for legitimate work.

## What you should be able to do

- Explain how hashing, encryption and digital signatures serve different purposes.
- Distinguish authentication factors, sessions and authorisation.
- Plan least-privilege access and explain how layered controls work together.

## Weekly checkpoint

Prepare: a hash-comparison explanation, a certificate observation and an access matrix with allowed and denied test cases.

Explain the purpose of your work, the evidence supporting the result and one limitation or unresolved question. Where you tested a control, distinguish the expected result from the observed result. Use fictional or approved training data and keep credentials out of submitted evidence.
