## Module overview

Turn scanner observations into validated findings, appropriate changes and defensible closure evidence.

## What you should be able to do

- Interpret scanner evidence and distinguish severity from business priority.
- Plan remediation with an owner, service checks and recovery route.
- Explain what a rescan does and does not verify.

## Preparation

Risk and compliance teaching in Week 17 completes the later prerequisites of Tool Lab 05 and Project 05. Do not declare those entire activities complete early.

## Guided practice and independent work

Begin the technical stages of Tool Lab 05 and Project 05. Use instructor-prepared feeds and targets where available. Validate one assigned finding, propose or perform the authorised fix, and compare security and service checks. Revisit Project 02 remediation work.

Use the [tool-lab index](../../practicals/labs/README.md) and [project index](../../practicals/projects/README.md) to open the relevant brief. A stage started this week is not the same as a completed lab or project. Record completed, deferred and not-run tasks accurately.

## Weekly checkpoint

Prepare: a finding worksheet, remediation record, rescan comparison and service-regression results.

Explain the purpose of your work, the evidence supporting the result and one limitation or unresolved question. Where you tested a control, distinguish the expected result from the observed result. Use fictional or approved training data and keep credentials out of submitted evidence.
