## Module overview

Build testable detection logic and connect findings to authorised response decisions.

## What you should be able to do

- Define a detection hypothesis and its data requirements.
- Test expected matches and ordinary activity.
- Distinguish containment, eradication and recovery decisions.

## Preparation

Do not automate disruptive response actions merely to finish a lesson. Use the assigned procedure and approved training targets.

## Guided practice and independent work

Use the detection lesson samples and approved benign tests to assess rule behaviour. Continue Tool Lab 06 with investigation and response reasoning. Practise an incident handover using a supplied case, including unknowns and the next authorised action.

Use the [tool-lab index](../../practicals/labs/README.md) and [project index](../../practicals/projects/README.md) to open the relevant brief. A stage started this week is not the same as a completed lab or project. Record completed, deferred and not-run tasks accurately.

## Weekly checkpoint

Prepare: a detection test record, triage note and incident decision log.

Explain the purpose of your work, the evidence supporting the result and one limitation or unresolved question. Where you tested a control, distinguish the expected result from the observed result. Use fictional or approved training data and keep credentials out of submitted evidence.
