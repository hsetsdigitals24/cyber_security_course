## Module overview

Follow events from their source into central search and alerting, and verify the evidence path.

## What you should be able to do

- Explain collection, parsing, normalisation and correlation.
- Confirm required event sources and agent health.
- Trace a harmless generated event into the monitoring platform.

## Preparation

The instructor must provide a prepared monitoring service or sufficient staged capacity. Incident response and intelligence stages follow Weeks 15–16.

## Guided practice and independent work

Begin Tool Lab 06 and the technical queue work of Project 06. Return to the central-monitoring requirements in Tool Labs 03 and 04 and Projects 03 and 04. Investigate missing telemetry before changing detection logic.

Use the [tool-lab index](../../practicals/labs/README.md) and [project index](../../practicals/projects/README.md) to open the relevant brief. A stage started this week is not the same as a completed lab or project. Record completed, deferred and not-run tasks accurately.

## Weekly checkpoint

Prepare: a source-to-platform trace, query evidence and a telemetry troubleshooting record.

Explain the purpose of your work, the evidence supporting the result and one limitation or unresolved question. Where you tested a control, distinguish the expected result from the observed result. Use fictional or approved training data and keep credentials out of submitted evidence.
