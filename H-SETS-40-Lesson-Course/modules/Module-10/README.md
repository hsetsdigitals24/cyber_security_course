## Module overview

Control how remote users and network segments reach services, while retaining a working management route.

## What you should be able to do

- Explain VPN routing, authentication and remote-access limits.
- Describe stateful filtering, NAT and firewall rule direction.
- Design and test narrowly scoped allowed and denied flows.

## Preparation

The assigned router/firewall VM and isolated segments must be prepared. Do not treat a successful existing session as the only rule test.

## Guided practice and independent work

Begin Tool Lab 03 interface, routing and firewall stages in the assigned pfSense range. Record a traffic matrix and preserve the management path. Start the design stages of Project 03; segmentation, discovery and central logging are developed in later weeks.

Use the [tool-lab index](../../practicals/labs/README.md) and [project index](../../practicals/projects/README.md) to open the relevant brief. A stage started this week is not the same as a completed lab or project. Record completed, deferred and not-run tasks accurately.

## Weekly checkpoint

Prepare: a remote-access explanation, firewall configuration evidence and a repeatable traffic test table.

Explain the purpose of your work, the evidence supporting the result and one limitation or unresolved question. Where you tested a control, distinguish the expected result from the observed result. Use fictional or approved training data and keep credentials out of submitted evidence.
