## Module overview

Apply controlled Linux changes and write small scripts whose results and failures you can explain.

## What you should be able to do

- Harden the assigned service without losing required access.
- Explain variables, decisions, loops and error handling.
- Test a small script against normal and invalid inputs.

## Preparation

Verify the Week 4 recovery baseline before changes. Complete every required Tool Lab 01 task before claiming that lab is finished.

## Guided practice and independent work

Complete the Bash and scripting stages of Tool Lab 01. Begin the applicable Linux hardening stages of Tool Lab 02 and Project 01. Retain any tasks requiring later endpoint, networking or remediation teaching as pending.

Use the [tool-lab index](../../practicals/labs/README.md) and [project index](../../practicals/projects/README.md) to open the relevant brief. A stage started this week is not the same as a completed lab or project. Record completed, deferred and not-run tasks accurately.

## Weekly checkpoint

Prepare: a tested beginner script, a before/after hardening record and Project 01 evidence in progress.

Explain the purpose of your work, the evidence supporting the result and one limitation or unresolved question. Where you tested a control, distinguish the expected result from the observed result. Use fictional or approved training data and keep credentials out of submitted evidence.
